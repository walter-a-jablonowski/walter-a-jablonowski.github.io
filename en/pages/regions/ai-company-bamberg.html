Which businesses in Bamberg benefit from AI?

Mostly those with many recurring, document- or data-driven processes. In Bamberg and Upper Franconia these are typically automotive and mechanical engineering suppliers, the brewing industry and its surroundings, medical technology around Forchheim, trade and crafts, law firms as well as hotels and tourism around the world heritage site. Whether AI pays off for you is what the free initial call is for.

What does an AI project cost?

Small projects are done at a fixed price, larger ones by effort; ongoing usage costs for the models come on top and stay transparent. A first automation starts at €1,000. After the free initial call you get a concrete estimate for your use case.

How quickly is something ready to use?

For a clearly scoped use case there is usually a first working version within a few weeks that you can try in day-to-day work. How long the full integration takes depends on the interfaces of your existing systems – which is why I prefer to start small and extend what proves itself.

Is the work done on-site or online?

Both. My base is in Stegaurach, around 20 minutes from Bamberg – so I am quickly with you for a meeting. Most of a project runs reliably online, and you have the same point of contact throughout instead of a changing agency team.

What happens to our company data?

You decide what leaves the house. On request the models run locally or in your own cloud, so no document goes to an external provider. Access and decisions are logged, and the GDPR questions are settled before the first line of code.
