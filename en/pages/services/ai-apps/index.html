Good to know

|

AI doesn't have to be a chat window. It pays off most where the work already happens: inside the application itself. The agent gets the same functions an employee operates by click – so it can do real work instead of just producing text.

The interface still matters: you see what happened, check results and correct where needed. This works as a new app or as an extension of an existing one. Thanks to AI-assisted development, building it is far faster and cheaper today than a few years ago.

What does such an app cost?

Small applications can be scoped well, larger ones are billed by effort. The concrete scope needs to be assessed individually in a consultation.

Do I have to replace my existing software?

No. AI features can be built into existing applications, or added as a separate interface next to them that works on the same data.

How is this different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers. An integrated agent works: it uses the same functions you do – create, change, move, evaluate – and the result is then visible in the application.

Do I stay in control of what the AI does?

Yes. Every action is visible in the interface and can be corrected. Critical steps can require an approval before they are carried out.

Where does the application run?

Locally or on your own server if you prefer – your data stays under your control. I support a GDPR-compliant implementation.

Can we start small?

Yes, that is the normal case: first a small, working version for one workflow – then views, data sources and AI capabilities are added step by step.

Why work with me?

An app with an agent needs both: solid software engineering and hands-on AI experience. I have been building applications for over 25 years and use AI every day – including for the development itself.
